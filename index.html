Housing & utility assistance

Help with a home, rent, or bills

If money is tight, there may be programs that can help. Start with the options below and check directly with each organization for current funding and eligibility.

Take one step at a time. You can contact an organization and ask what documents are needed before you apply. You do not have to do everything today.

💧 Water bill assistance — Economic Security Corporation (ESC)

Economic Security Corporation of Southwest Area serves communities in southwest Missouri. Its assistance programs and service area may depend on the program and current funding. Ask ESC whether water-bill help is currently available for your county and water provider.

Visit Economic Security Corporation →

Confirm eligibility, funding, service area, and application steps directly with ESC. Listing does not guarantee assistance.

🏡 Affordable housing and rental support

For federal housing information and links to local public housing agencies, start with the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development.

⚡ Electric, gas, and energy bills

The Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP) helps eligible households with energy costs. Availability and applications are handled by state and local agencies.

Learn about LIHEAP →

Our promise

Free to use. No judgment. No shame.

Blair's Helping Hand does not charge visitors to use this website or find listed resources. We want information to be easier to understand and easier to reach.

Please remember: Blair's Helping Hand is an information and guidance project, not a government agency or a provider of financial assistance. We cannot guarantee that a program has funding or that an application will be approved. Always use the official organization’s application and contact details.
